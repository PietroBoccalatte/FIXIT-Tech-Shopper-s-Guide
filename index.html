🛠️ Maintenance & Longevity Tips

  • Keep it cool: Heat is the enemy of electronics. Use your device on surfaces that allow air circulation, avoid direct sunlight, and keep vents clear.
  • Battery care: Keep your battery between 20-80%. On newer devices you can optimise battery charging to increase durability and perfromance
  • Regular software updates: Keep your devices up to date to make sure they run smoothly and are safe from malware
  • Use a protective case: Cases and screen protectors are cheap and easy to install, use them
  • Clean dust filters: On laptops and desktops, dust buildup reduces cooling efficiency. Use compressed air quarterly on vents.
  • Right-to-repair your device: Learn how to replace a battery, screen, or storage. iFixit and manufacturer guides make it accessible.
  • Buy refurbished parts: Screen protectors, batteries, and charging cables sourced used/refurbished further reduce e-waste and cost.
